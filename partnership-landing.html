Why This Partnership Matters

Together, We Honor the People Who Serve.

Flags USA and the USO share a commitment to the men and women who serve this nation. A flag is more than a product. It's a symbol of what we stand for and who we stand with. This partnership lets every purchase mean something more.

For 85 years the USO has stood beside service members from the moment they enlist through every stage of service. By connecting Flags USA customers with that mission, we can directly support troops and military families in a way that is personal and real.

The USO's Reach

Wherever Troops Serve, the USO Is There.

The USO operates in 55 U.S. states and territories and across 134 countries, reaching service members in combat zones, isolated postings, and everywhere in between. They've been doing it since 1941.

Your purchase and donation go directly to USO programs: care packages, entertainment tours, transition support, family resources, and more. It's support that reaches troops wherever they are.
